I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at [mediaire GmbH](https://mediaire.de), leading AI development for MRI-based medical image analysis products, and a Visiting Researcher at the Diagnostic Image Analysis Group (DIAG) at [Radboud University Medical Center](https://www.radboudumc.nl/en), working on AI for prostate cancer diagnosis.

I was previously a a Doctoral Researcher at the [German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ)](https://www.dkfz.de/en/index.html) and pursued a Ph.D. in Computer Science at [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/), on *Transformers and Self-Attention for Medical Image Segmentation.* One of the key contributions of my Ph.D. research is the development of **MedNeXt**, a 3D ConvNeXt-based architecture for medical image segmentation, which has been widely adopted **(700+ citations)** in the research community and has won multiple benchmark evaluations **(NeurIPS 2024, MICCAI 2024)** as an improvement upon the popular **nnUNet**.

During my Ph.D. I also spent time as a Research Intern at [Siemens Healthineers, USA](https://www.siemens-healthineers.com) developing patch-based diffusion models for medical image denoising and super-resolution. Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en), working alongside the [German Center for Neurodegenerative Diseases (DZNE)](https://www.dzne.de/en) on 3D deep representation learning for neuroanatomy segmentation.

My research specializes on predictive and generative problems in 3D medical image analysis, particularly focusing on effective large-scale 3D representation learning.
